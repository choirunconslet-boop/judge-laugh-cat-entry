A one-star review written by a house cat about its human: a deadpan complaint about an empty food bowl, escalated the way only a cat would — straight to management (itself) and a 3 a.m. screaming campaign until morale improves. `main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

> "One star. The food bowl was empty for four whole minutes. I have informed management (me) and will be screaming at 3 a.m. until morale improves."
